Baked Champignons (with cheese) / ქამა სოკო კეცზე სულგუნით

Ingredients

  • Champignons - 4-8, medium-large sized
  • Butter - 3 grams per Champignon
  • Cheese (სულგუნი or Mozzarella) - 5 grams per Champignon
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Pre-heat the oven at 250°C.
  2. Clean the Champignons and remove the stems (either by cutting or breaking them off).
  3. Lay the champignons upside down on a pan that you're gonna cook them with.
  4. Slice the butter and the cheese into small pieces.
  5. Salt the mushrooms. Do this by your taste.
  6. Put slices of about 3 grams of butter and about 5 grams of cheese in each mushroom. You can judge by size. Keep in mind: the cheese will melt.
  7. Cook for about 10-15 minutes. The mushrooms should turn brown and the cheese should start getting crusty and golden-brown on the top.
